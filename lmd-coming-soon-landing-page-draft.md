# CoolNerdz Coming Soon Landing Page Draft

## Concept

Create a polished coming-soon landing page for CoolNerdz as one connected ecosystem for learning, progress, and wellbeing.

The page should not feel like a temporary placeholder. It should clearly tell visitors what is being built, who it is for, and why they should join the early-access list.

## Positioning

Headline:
A smarter way to learn, grow, and feel supported.

Subheadline:
A new platform for courses, AI-assisted teaching, student progress, athlete development, and counselor-supported care is coming soon.

Primary CTA:
Join the waitlist

Secondary CTA:
View what is coming

Audience line:
For students, teachers, athletes, counselors, academies, and growing learning teams.

## Visual Direction

Style:
Modern, calm, practical, and premium enough for client approval.

Palette:
- Warm off-white background
- White product surfaces
- Dark stone text
- Sage and soft blue for learning/progress
- Soft coral for key highlights
- Light lavender for wellness moments

Typography:
Clean rounded sans-serif such as Outfit or Inter. Use sentence case. No decorative fonts.

UI feel:
Subtle borders, 8px cards, quiet shadows, realistic product mockups, and no loud neon or generic stock-photo hero.

## Page Structure

### 1. Navigation

Logo/wordmark:
CoolNerdz

Links:
Platform, For teachers, Wellness app, Counselors, Coming soon

CTA:
Join waitlist

Design:
A compact floating nav with white background, soft border, and clear mobile menu behavior.

### 2. Hero

Hero should show the product immediately, not just text.

Left side:
- Coming soon badge
- Main headline
- Short supporting copy
- Two CTAs
- Audience trust line

Right side:
Layered interface preview:
- LMS dashboard
- AI course builder panel
- Student progress tracker
- Mobile counselor appointment screen

Goal:
Make the client feel this is already becoming a real product.

### 3. Audience Strip

Show six role chips:
- Students: learn with clear progress
- Teachers: create courses faster with AI
- Athletes: follow development programs
- Counselors: manage bookings and support
- Academies: organize learning paths
- Organizations: track growth and outcomes

### 4. What We Are Building

Two major panels:

AI-powered LMS:
- Sell and manage courses
- AI-assisted course creation
- Student progress management
- Learning paths and milestones
- Athlete development programs

Calm mobile support app:
- Calm and grounding tools
- Book appointments with counselors
- Counselor signup and profiles
- Guided support journeys
- Mobile-first care access

### 5. Role-Based Journeys

Use segmented tabs:
Student, Teacher, Athlete, Counselor

Each selected role changes the preview:
- Student: course list, weekly goal, progress path
- Teacher: AI module builder, content outline, learner tracking
- Athlete: program milestones, coach feedback, performance learning
- Counselor: availability, booking requests, session overview

### 6. AI Course Builder Preview

Section headline:
Teachers stay in control. AI handles the heavy starting work.

Interface details:
- Prompt box: "Create a 6-week beginner course..."
- Generated module outline
- Edit/reorder controls
- Lesson status labels
- Publish course button

### 7. Progress Management

Show dashboard-style proof:
- Progress rings
- Milestone cards
- Learner status list
- At-risk / on-track signals
- Teacher or coach insight panel

Copy angle:
Progress should be visible, measurable, and easy to act on.

### 8. Mobile Wellness Preview

Three phone screens:
- Calm: breathing session and mood check-in
- Counselors: search, profile card, availability
- Booking: date/time picker and confirmation

Keep this section soft, but still product-specific.

### 9. Coming Soon Roadmap

Timeline:
- Now: LMS foundation and course marketplace
- Next: AI course creation and progress management
- Then: mobile calm tools and counselor booking
- Later: communities, analytics, and expanded programs

### 10. Waitlist CTA

Headline:
Be first to see the platform when it opens.

Form:
- Email input
- Role dropdown: Student, Teacher, Athlete, Counselor, Organization
- Submit button: Join waitlist

Note:
Early access updates only. No spam.

### 11. Footer

Simple footer with:
- CoolNerdz wordmark
- Product links
- Contact
- Privacy placeholder
- Line: coolnerdz.com · Built for learning, growth, and care.

## Client Presentation Notes

The strongest message is that CoolNerdz is not just an LMS and not just a wellness app. It is an ecosystem around learning outcomes, personal growth, and support.

The landing page should lead with clarity:
- What is coming
- Who it is for
- What each product does
- How visitors can get early access

## Recommended First Prototype Direction

Use the hero as the main selling point:
A calm, modern page with a realistic multi-product interface preview. Show the LMS dashboard and AI course builder as the core product, then introduce the mobile wellness/counselor app as the support layer that makes the ecosystem broader and more human.
